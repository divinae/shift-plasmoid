**Description** (each line its own paragraph)

```
Sit less, without thinking about it. A Plasma 6 widget that reminds you to switch between sitting and standing, counts your standing time against a goal, and can move a motorized desk for you.

A cycle that runs itself: 45 minutes sitting, 15 standing by default, with a notification when it's time to switch (Sit/Stand now, Snooze).

Moves the desk for you: IKEA Idåsen and other Linak desks through idasen, or any command line tool. It notices when you use the desk's own buttons too.

Progress you can see: daily standing time against a goal, a streak of days that met it, and the week as a chart.

Polite about your day: paused while the screen is locked, Pause for a meeting, optional working hours, and reminders that wait for Do Not Disturb to end.

Middle-click the panel icon to switch without opening the popup.

Source, issues and the full README (desk setup included): https://github.com/divinae/shift-plasmoid

Support: https://ko-fi.com/divinae4535
Made with AI: most of the code is written with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant. I decide what goes in and review every change before a release.
```
